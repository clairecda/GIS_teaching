# Background Reading

These readings provide foundational knowledge for the course. Complete them before the indicated week to get the most out of class.

---

## 1. What is GIS?

**Read before:** Week 1 | **Time:** 15 minutes

Your first introduction to Geographic Information Systems. Learn what GIS is, why location matters, the difference between vector and raster data, and how GIS is used in everyday life.

[Read: What is GIS?](week01-what-is-gis.md)

---

## 2. Understanding Coordinate Reference Systems

**Read before:** Week 1 (skim) / Week 3 (detail) | **Time:** 30 minutes

The most confusing topic for GIS beginners — explained clearly. Covers why layers don't line up, when to use geographic vs projected CRS, common EPSG codes, and how to fix CRS problems in QGIS.

[Read: Understanding CRS](understanding-crs.md)

---

## 3. Administrative Boundaries

**Read before:** Week 3 | **Time:** 20 minutes

What are SA2s, LGAs, and census tracts? This reading explains boundary hierarchies, the Modifiable Areal Unit Problem (MAUP), how to join data correctly, and common mistakes to avoid.

[Read: Administrative Boundaries](week03-admin-boundaries.md)

---

## 4. Raster Data Basics

**Read before:** Week 4 | **Time:** 15 minutes

Introduction to grid-based spatial data. Covers DEM vs DSM vs DTM, satellite imagery bands, resolution trade-offs, and common raster operations in QGIS.

[Read: Raster Data Basics](week04-raster-basics.md)

---

## 5. Ethics in Spatial Analysis

**Read before:** Week 5 | **Time:** 20 minutes

Maps have power — they can help or harm communities. This reading covers privacy, data bias, the ecological fallacy, crime mapping ethics, and responsible approaches to mapping sensitive data.

[Read: Ethics in Spatial Analysis](week05-ethics-in-mapping.md)

---

## 6. Python for GIS

**Read before:** Week 7 | **Time:** 15 minutes

Why learn to code? This reading introduces the Python GIS ecosystem (GeoPandas, Rasterio, OSMnx), shows what Python code looks like, and explains Jupyter notebooks. Prepares you for the transition from clicking to coding.

[Read: Python for GIS](week07-python-for-gis.md)

---
