# Background Reading

These readings provide foundational knowledge for the course. Read them before the indicated week.

---

## 1. What is GIS?

**Read before:** Week 1

**Time:** 15 minutes

An introduction to Geographic Information Systems — what they are, how they work, and why they matter. Covers the difference between spatial and tabular data, common applications, and key terminology you'll use throughout the course.

[Read: What is GIS?](week01-what-is-gis.md)

---

## 2. Understanding Coordinate Reference Systems

**Read before:** Week 1 (skim), Week 3 (in depth)

**Time:** 20 minutes

Why do map layers sometimes not line up? What's the difference between WGS84 and Web Mercator? This guide explains coordinate reference systems, projections, and when to use each. Essential for avoiding common mistakes.

[Read: Understanding CRS](understanding-crs.md)

---

## 3. Administrative Boundaries

**Read before:** Week 3

**Time:** 15 minutes

What are SA2s, LGAs, and census tracts? Why do they matter for analysis? This reading explains how administrative and statistical boundaries work, why they change over time, and how to choose the right level for your project.

[Read: Administrative Boundaries](week03-admin-boundaries.md)
