# Background Reading

These readings provide foundational knowledge for the course. Complete them before the indicated week to get the most out of class.

---

## 1. What is GIS?

**Read before:** Week 1 | **Time:** 15 minutes

Your first introduction to Geographic Information Systems. Learn what GIS is, why location matters, the difference between vector and raster data, and how GIS is used in everyday life. Includes key terms you'll use throughout the course.

[Read: What is GIS?](week01-what-is-gis.md)

---

## 2. Understanding Coordinate Reference Systems

**Read before:** Week 1 (skim) / Week 3 (in depth) | **Time:** 30 minutes

The most confusing topic for GIS beginners — explained clearly. Covers why layers don't line up, when to use geographic vs projected CRS, common EPSG codes for Australia and globally, and how to fix CRS problems in QGIS.

[Read: Understanding CRS](understanding-crs.md)

---

## 3. Administrative Boundaries

**Read before:** Week 3 | **Time:** 20 minutes

What are SA2s, LGAs, and census tracts? Why do they change over time? This reading explains the hierarchy of administrative and statistical boundaries, the Modifiable Areal Unit Problem (MAUP), how to join data correctly, and common mistakes to avoid.

[Read: Administrative Boundaries](week03-admin-boundaries.md)
