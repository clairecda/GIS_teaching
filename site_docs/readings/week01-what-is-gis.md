# What Is GIS?

Start Week 1 by exploring two short resources that introduce core GIS ideas and real-world applications:

1. **Read:** [Esri — What Is GIS?](https://www.esri.com/en-us/what-is-gis/overview).  
   Focus on sections covering spatial relationships, data models, and everyday examples. Note any terms you want to unpack during the orientation session.
2. **Watch:** [GIS Explained in 5 Minutes (YouTube)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WpoSofhf9Y0).  
   This video offers a concise overview; jot down one insight that surprised you or one question it sparked.

After engaging with both, capture your takeaways in the Week 1 reflection:

- What is the most compelling example you encountered?
- Which GIS terms (vector, raster, spatial analysis) feel familiar vs. new?
- How do you already use or notice GIS in daily life?

Bring these notes to Week 1’s spatial thinking icebreaker.
