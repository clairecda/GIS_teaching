## Week-by-Week

### Weeks 1-6: QGIS Foundations

| Week | Topic | You'll Learn | Capstone Task |
|:----:|-------|--------------|---------------|
| 1 | **QGIS Orientation** | Interface, loading data, basic styling | Brainstorm topics |
| 2 | **Symbology & Layouts** | Thematic maps, print layouts | Plan visualisation |
| 3 | **Vector Analysis** | Spatial joins, attribute queries | **Choose study area** |
| 4 | **Raster & Terrain** | DEMs, hillshades, calculations | Identify datasets |
| 5 | **Crime Mapping** | KDE, hotspots, ethics | **Refine question** |
| 6 | **Health Access** | Networks, service areas | Complete data collection |

### Weeks 7-10: Python for Spatial Analysis

| Week | Topic | You'll Learn | Capstone Task |
|:----:|-------|--------------|---------------|
| 7 | **Bridge to Python** | Jupyter, pandas, GeoPandas | **Data ready** |
| 8 | **Vector Workflows** | Spatial joins, automation | Begin analysis |
| 9 | **Remote Sensing** | Rasterio, NDVI, change detection | Add satellite data? |
| 10 | **Networks** | OSMnx, isochrones, routing | **Draft maps** |

### Weeks 11-12: Synthesis

| Week | Topic | You'll Learn | Capstone Task |
|:----:|-------|--------------|---------------|
| 11 | **Design & Story** | Critique, accessibility, narrative | Polish & feedback |
| 12 | **Capstone** | Presentation, documentation | **Final submission** |

- [ ] **Week 7:** All datasets collected, folder structure ready
- [ ] **Week 10:** Draft maps created, analysis mostly complete
- [ ] **Week 12:** Final project submitted and presented
