# Reflection Prompts

Use these prompts to capture weekly takeaways, blockers, and questions. Copy them into your notes or version-controlled journal.

## Week 1 — QGIS Orientation

- What worked well during installation and first project setup?
- Where do you still feel unsure navigating the QGIS interface?
- List any issues to raise during the support clinic.

## Week 2 — Symbology & Layouts

- Which design choices (colour, labelling, layout) are you most proud of?
- How will you iterate on accessibility for the next map export?
- What questions remain about the styling tools or print composer?

## Week 3 — Vector Analysis & Joins

- Which attribute or spatial query unlocked the most insight this week?
- How confident do you feel using the Field Calculator and selections?
- Note any datasets you plan to bring into the crime case study.

## Week 4 — Raster & Terrain

- What raster styling or analysis technique felt most approachable?
- Where do you still need support with reprojection or interpolation?
- Capture ideas for applying these techniques to environmental resilience topics.

## Week 5 — Crime Mapping

- How did hotspot tools change your understanding of the data?
- What contextual information would make your map more actionable?
- List follow-up tasks to refine temporal filters or storytelling elements.

## Week 6 — Public Health & Accessibility

- Which network or service-area insight felt most meaningful for equity analysis?
- Document any friction encountered with plugins or data availability.
- Identify questions to bring into the Python bridge regarding reproducibility.

## Week 7 — Bridge to Python

- What part of the managed environment setup was clearest, and what needs clarification?
- How ready do you feel to translate QGIS habits into scripted workflows?
- List dependencies or datasets you want packaged before diving into Python.

## Week 8 — Python Vector Workflows

- Which step translated most naturally from QGIS into Python?
- Where did Python automation save you time or introduce complexity?
- List questions to bring to the reproducibility clinic next session.

## Week 9 — Raster & Remote Sensing in Python

- How well did the raster workflows translate from QGIS into code?
- What questions remain about band math, masking, or zonal statistics?
- Capture automation ideas to bring into the capstone project.

## Week 10 — Transport Networks

- Which part of the network pipeline felt most valuable?
- Note any data gaps or assumptions that could impact conclusions.
- Identify enhancements to test before Week 11’s design studio.

## Week 11 — Design & Storytelling

- What feedback did you receive on layout, typography, or narrative flow?
- How will you adapt your map for accessibility (colour contrast, alt text, annotations)?
- List final tasks required to polish your capstone deliverables.

## Week 12 — Capstone Showcase

- Summarise the core question your project answered and the key takeaway.
- What feedback did you receive from peers or stakeholders?
- Document next steps for sharing your work (blog post, GitHub README, portfolio update).
