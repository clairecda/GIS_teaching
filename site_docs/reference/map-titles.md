# Writing Effective Map Titles

A good title helps readers understand your map before they even look at the data. This guide covers title structure, common mistakes, and examples.

## The Title Formula

**Structure:** [What] + [Where] + [When] + optional [Why/So What]

| Component | Purpose | Example |
|-----------|---------|---------|
| **What** | The phenomenon being mapped | Population Density |
| **Where** | Geographic scope | Greater Sydney |
| **When** | Time period of data | 2021 |
| **Why/So What** | Interpretive hook (optional) | Revealing Urban Sprawl Patterns |

---

## Examples: Before and After

### Example 1: Demographic Map

| Before | After |
|--------|-------|
| "Population Map" | "Population Density in Greater Sydney, 2021" |
| | *Better:* "Uneven Growth: Population Density Shifts in Greater Sydney, 2016-2021" |

**Why it's better:** Specific location, time period, and interpretive framing.

---

### Example 2: Transport Analysis

| Before | After |
|--------|-------|
| "Bus Routes" | "Public Transit Coverage in Melbourne's Western Suburbs" |
| | *Better:* "Transit Deserts: Areas Beyond 400m Walking Distance to Bus Stops, Melbourne West" |

**Why it's better:** Specifies the phenomenon (coverage gaps), location, and implies the analytical finding.

---

### Example 3: Environmental Map

| Before | After |
|--------|-------|
| "Flood Risk" | "Flood-Prone Areas in Brisbane River Catchment" |
| | *Better:* "1-in-100 Year Flood Extent: Properties at Risk in Brisbane River Catchment, 2024 Update" |

**Why it's better:** Specifies the flood scenario, what's at risk, and data currency.

---

### Example 4: Health Access

| Before | After |
|--------|-------|
| "Healthcare Facilities" | "Hospital Accessibility in Rural NSW" |
| | *Better:* "Beyond Reach: Populations More Than 30 Minutes from Emergency Care, Rural NSW" |

**Why it's better:** Frames the issue (access gaps), specifies the threshold, and targets the affected group.

## Common Mistakes

### 1. Too Vague

- "Land Use Map"
- "Crime Analysis"
- "Environmental Data"

**Problem:** Could apply to thousands of maps. Provides no useful information.

### 2. Too Technical

- "SEIFA IRSD Decile Quintiles by SA2"
- "Kernel Density Estimation Output (h=500m)"

**Problem:** Jargon excludes non-specialist audiences. Define acronyms or use plain language.

### 3. Missing Context

- "Melbourne 2021"
- "NSW Health Facilities"

**Problem:** What about Melbourne? What aspect of health facilities?

### 4. Too Long

- "A Comprehensive Analysis of the Spatial Distribution of Socioeconomic Disadvantage as Measured by the Australian Bureau of Statistics Index of Relative Socio-Economic Disadvantage Across Statistical Area Level 2 Boundaries in Greater Melbourne for the 2021 Census Year"

**Problem:** Titles should fit on one line. Move details to subtitles or captions.

---

## Title Structure Options

### Option 1: Descriptive (Neutral)

**Format:** [Topic] in [Location], [Date]

> "Population Density in Greater Sydney, 2021"

**Best for:** Reference maps, technical reports, data documentation.

---

### Option 2: Interpretive (Engaging)

**Format:** [Insight]: [Specific Description]

> "Urban Sprawl Accelerates: Population Growth Hotspots in Sydney's Outer Suburbs, 2016-2021"

**Best for:** Presentations, journalism, advocacy, storytelling.

---

### Option 3: Question-Based

**Format:** [Question the map answers]

> "Where Are Melbourne's Transit Deserts?"

**Best for:** Exploratory analysis, community engagement, educational materials.

---

## Using Subtitles

For complex maps, use a main title plus subtitle:

**Main title:** Short, attention-grabbing (5-10 words)

**Subtitle:** Additional context, methods, or data source (10-20 words)

**Example:**

> **Uneven Recovery**
> *Employment Change by Suburb in Greater Brisbane, March 2020 to March 2023*

---

## Title Checklist

Before finalizing your title, verify:

- [ ] **Specific:** Could this title only apply to YOUR map?
- [ ] **Located:** Is the geographic scope clear?
- [ ] **Dated:** Is the time period specified?
- [ ] **Jargon-free:** Would a non-specialist understand every word?
- [ ] **Accurate:** Does the title match what the map actually shows?
- [ ] **Concise:** Can it fit on one line at readable size?

## Quick Reference

| Map Type | Title Template |
|----------|---------------|
| Choropleth | "[Variable] in [Location], [Date]" |
| Change map | "[Variable] Change in [Location], [Start]-[End]" |
| Hotspot analysis | "[Phenomenon] Hotspots in [Location]" |
| Accessibility | "[Service] Access in [Location]: [Threshold] Analysis" |
| Comparison | "[Location A] vs [Location B]: [Variable Compared]" |

---

## Test Your Title

Ask someone unfamiliar with your project:

1. "Based on this title, what do you expect to see on the map?"
2. "What's the main message or finding?"
3. "Who do you think would find this map useful?"

If their answers match your intentions, your title works. If not, revise.
