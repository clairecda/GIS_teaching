# Introduction to GIS

Welcome to a 12-week, project-based journey through Geographic Information Systems. This course begins with hands-on QGIS skills and gradually transitions into Python-based analysis, helping you move from point-and-click workflows to reproducible code.

---

## Course structure

| Weeks | Focus | Tools |
|-------|-------|-------|
| 1-6 | GIS foundations | QGIS |
| 7 | Bridge to Python | Anaconda, Jupyter |
| 8-10 | Python automation | GeoPandas, Rasterio, OSMnx |
| 11-12 | Design & capstone | QGIS + Python |

**Capstone project:** You'll work on a spatial project throughout all 12 weeks, not just at the end. Start thinking about your topic in Week 1.

## Who this course is for

- Students new to spatial analysis and mapping
- Researchers working with geographic data
- Professionals needing GIS skills for planning, health, environment, or policy work
- Self-learners building a portfolio in geospatial analysis

**Prerequisites:** No prior GIS or programming experience required. Basic computer skills assumed (file management, installing software).

---

## Your responsibilities

This course requires **initiative and preparation**. Success depends on you taking ownership of your learning.

### Before each class

- Complete the readings and download the required data
- Set up your folder structure and verify your software works
- **If you have issues, contact the teaching team BEFORE class** — we cannot troubleshoot installations or missing data during lab time

### For your capstone project

- **Discuss your project idea with the teaching team early** — don't wait until Week 10
- Come with a topic area and initial questions; we'll help you refine them
- Try to find data sources yourself first, then ask for guidance if stuck
- Check in at milestones (Weeks 3, 5, 7, 10) to stay on track

### Asking for help

We're here to support you, but we expect you to:

1. **Try first** — attempt the problem, read error messages, check the documentation
2. **Be specific** — "It doesn't work" isn't helpful; "I get this error when I try to join these tables" is
3. **Come prepared** — have your data ready, your project open, and your question clear

!!! tip "The teaching team wants you to succeed"
    We're happy to help with guidance, troubleshooting, and feedback — but the learning happens when *you* do the work. Take initiative, ask questions, and engage with the material.

---

## Learning outcomes

By the end of the course you will be able to:

1. Use QGIS confidently for mapping and spatial analysis
2. Understand coordinate systems, projections, and data formats
3. Join data to boundaries and create thematic maps
4. Perform hotspot analysis, accessibility modelling, and terrain analysis
5. Automate workflows with Python and GeoPandas
6. Design accessible, publication-ready maps
7. Complete a portfolio-ready capstone project
