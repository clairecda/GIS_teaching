# Week 11 Lecture · Narrative Map Design

## Objectives

- Reinforce storytelling principles for spatial narratives (setup → insight → call to action).
- Integrate graphic design techniques (hierarchy, rhythm, visual anchors).
- Emphasise accessibility and ethical communication in final outputs.

## Outline

1. **Narrative structure**  
   - Hook, context, insight, recommendation.  
   - Choosing the appropriate medium (static map, story map, slide deck).
2. **Design systems**  
   - Grid-based layout, consistent typography, supporting graphics (charts, icons).  
   - Borrowing best practices from magazine spreads and infographics.
3. **Accessibility & inclusion**  
   - Colour contrast testing, alternative text, accessible PDFs.  
   - Plain language summaries alongside visuals.
4. **Critique framework**  
   - Provide rubric for peer review (clarity, accuracy, aesthetics, accessibility).  
   - Encourage iterative feedback loops.

## References

## Activities

- Analyse a high-impact map story and identify design choices that support the narrative.  
- Peer critique sessions using the rubric before the design studio lab.
