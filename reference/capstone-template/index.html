    Capstone Proposal Template¶

    Use this template to plan your capstone project. Complete each section thoughtfully; this document becomes your roadmap for Week 12.

    Project Overview¶

    Project title:

    Your name:

    Date:

    1. Research Question¶

    Write one clear sentence describing the spatial question you're trying to answer.

    Checklist for a good research question:

    • Focuses on a specific geographic area (not too broad)
    • Can be answered with available data
    • Involves spatial analysis (not just displaying data on a map)
    • Is meaningful to you, your field, or your community

    2. Background & Motivation¶

    Why does this question matter? Who would benefit from knowing the answer? (2-4 sentences)

    What analysis techniques will you use? Check all that apply and describe your workflow.

    Techniques from the course:¶

    • Raster analysis / terrain modeling (Week 4)

    • Kernel density estimation (Week 5)

    Describe the analysis steps in order (e.g., "1. Download census boundaries. 2. Join population data. 3. Create 15-minute service areas around hospitals. 4. Calculate population within/outside service areas.")

    8. Feasibility Check¶

    Be honest with yourself about scope.

    Can you complete this in the available time?

    • Yes, confident
    • Probably, with focused effort
    • Uncertain; may need to reduce scope
    • No; need to simplify

    Do you have all required skills?

    • Yes, covered in course
    • Mostly; need to review [specific topic]: ___
    • Will need to learn [new skill]: ___

    Is the scope appropriate?

    • Might be too narrow (consider expanding)
    • About right
    • Might be too broad (consider narrowing)

    If you need to reduce scope, what would you cut?
